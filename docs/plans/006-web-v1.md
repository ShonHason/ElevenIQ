## W4 — Player page
- **Identity and context.**
- **Profile shape (pre-registered by the user, 2026-10-09; built in W4, not before):** a polygon of the player's DNA family dimension scores. It has one vertex per DNA family of the player's position group, on a 0–100 radial scale. The score is the exported mean local percentile of the family's directional members, lower-better as 100 − p (`analytics/dna.py`).
  - **Placement:** at the top of the player page, above the detailed radar and tables. On phones it replaces the bars-only fallback; the per-metric detail stays in the family tables.
  - **Withheld family:** an open, dashed vertex labelled "Withheld" (chip and reason from the words table). It is never drawn as 0, and the polygon closes over the scored vertices without it.
  - **No overall rating number.** The family weights are provisional until M5.
  - **Title** "Profile shape", plus a one-line note. The note says the shape shows how strongly the player stands out in each family within their league position group (local basis), and that it shows style, not a quality rating. The wording comes from the words table, and the stats-reviewer checks it. The pronoun is neutral ("the player's").
  - **Compare mode:** overlays two shapes, only when both players are in the same position group. Otherwise it shows two separate shapes, or none (a W4 decision at build time).
  - **Marks and colours:** per DESIGN.md and the dataviz skill. The 50 ring is marked as "around average"; there are `data-src` traces on every vertex and no animation.
  - **Added to `docs/design/preview.html` at W4** for the user's visual review.
  - **Facts at registration (2015/16 dimensions parquet):**
    - vertices per group: AM-Winger, DM-CM and FB-WB 7 (5 scored); ST 6 (5 scored); CB 5 (**3 scored**);
    - GK has no DNA, so no shape;
    - Defending is withheld in every group, and Passing in all but ST.
  - **Open points for the W4 plan, with the user's leanings (2026-10-09; decided at W4 with the preview):**
    1. The CB shape is a triangle with two withheld vertices. Leaning: show it with its two dashed withheld vertices, and include a CB example in the W4 preview so the user decides visually.
    2. The score is direction-adjusted ("more of the good end"). Leaning: the note says neither "style" nor "quality", e.g. "how strongly the player stands out in each family compared with players in the same position and league". The stats-reviewer checks it.
    3. Compare mode with mixed position groups. Leaning: two separate shapes side by side, no overlay. **Superseded (user, 2026-10-09):** no cross-group compare (W1 contract, compare mode).
  - **Tests:** a Playwright flow covers:
    - the withheld vertex is dashed, labelled and not at 0;
    - no overall number on the page;
    - compare mode with mixed groups shows no overlay;
    - the vertices trace to the fixture.
- **Radar:** shared-group rules from ADR 004 §12.9 (gaps for missing metrics, style axes uncoloured, `data-src` on every vertex); no animation.
- **Dimension scores:** with member counts and withheld status.
  - **Possession flags and withheld Defending (stats-reviewer N1–N3, M4g4d; user 2026-10-09):**
    - N1: a possession flag ("rises with own possession", "|β̂| > 1") is shown only with its bootstrap CI next to it, never alone. In 2015/16 only CB `ball_recoveries_p90` has a CI that excludes 0.
    - N2: `possession_criteria_failed` is worded only as "not shown to be free of team-possession effects", never as "possession-dependent". This also applies to any grounded or LLM text built from it.
    - N3 (narrowed by the user, 2026-10-09; ADR 004 §14 item 5): a withheld Defending score is never presented as a finding about the player. The second-season clause ("the expected state until a second season adds evidence") applies only to the mover-test groups (DM-CM in 2015/16: underpowered at 24). The possession-failed groups (CB, FB-WB, AM-Winger, ST) read "… not shown to be free of team-possession effects. This is not a finding about the player."
  - **Near-gate reliability (user, 2026-10-09; ADR 004 §14 sign-off note 3):** CB and FB-WB profiles with 900–1,350 nominal minutes are marked as less reliable (their DNA measures have a median r_full of about 0.60 in that bucket, below the 0.7 "lower reliability" line).
  - **Same-team neighbours (user, 2026-10-09; sign-off note 4):** neighbour lists mark a neighbour from the player's own team, so the teammate effect is visible (CB 2.17×, FB-WB 1.78× the expected rate).
- **Percentile tables per family:** the §11.5 display rules (>99 / <1, status text for nulls, the ratio population sentence, team-influenced wording, global / local basis); tabular figures.
- **Signature traits:** shown as holder / non-holder.
- **Links:** to Similar players and the style-map point.

## How later chunks interact
- **M4g3–M4g6:**
  - re-run the exporter (new `registry_version` stamp) and re-check with `spikes/w1_export_reference.py --check-output`;
  - frontend and Playwright fixtures unchanged;
  - a new status code needs a `labels.json` text, or the export fails;
  - a changed axis loading that breaks a quoted value fails the export until the user re-checks the names.
  - **Mover verdicts (M4g3b, requirement):** if the contract or a page carries a mover verdict, it always shows its flags next to it: "underpowered at n", "borderline (seed-sensitive)", "PASS (below model range)". Never a bare "PASS" (user, 2026-10-08, from the M4g3b output review: 4 of the 9 PASS verdicts are underpowered, and `op_xg_assisted_p90` is also below model range). The exporter takes the flags from the registry (`Derivation.movers`), never recomputes them, and the contract has no verdict field without its flag fields.
- **M5 (similarity engine):** the neighbour section of the contract is replaced by the M5 output (contract minor version if the fields hold, major if they change). The "provisional" label goes.
- **M7 (API):** `frontend/src/data/` switches from static fetches to the API serving the same contract; pages unchanged.
