### Method

- **Input:** complete primary scored rows per group; each local percentile / 100 times √(its per-metric family weight). Every complete pair's input distance equals `pair_distance` within 1e-12 (asserted). No standardisation.

- **Labels:** per-position DM-CM minutes on the ADR 002 §31 clock (the `verify_core_metrics` spike's implementation); their sum equals `minutes_dm_cm` for every DM-CM row (tolerance 1e-06). 6 / 8 and pivot labels at 0.7 (ADR 004 §13.4).
- **Neighbours:** top 10 M4f neighbours on the full vector (partial rows included), excluding the same player; ties by the season key.

**Display rule for naming an axis end (not pre-registered).** An end is described by its loadings of at least 0.15 (unrounded).
