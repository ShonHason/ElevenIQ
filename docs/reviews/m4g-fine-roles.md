# M4g — Fine-role sample sizes (report only)

User request 2026-10-07: measure before building anything. Generated by `spikes/m4g_fine_roles.py` (no eleveniq imports). Cohorts, percentiles and DNA are unchanged. Build: `registry_version` aaecf86fa483…, source `statsbomb`.

## Method

- **Rows:** the 1,233 primary scored rows (DNA rows) of the four 2015/16 pool leagues: gated (≥ 900 nominal minutes), outfield, `cohort_role` primary. One row per player-team-league-season; the group is the row's `position_group`.
- **Minutes per lineup position** on the ADR 002 §31 clock (the `verify_core_metrics` spike's implementation); their sum equals `minutes_played` for every row (tolerance 1e-6).
- **Label:** the fine role holding ≥ 0.7 of the row's minutes, else `mixed`; a mixed row's combo is its two largest roles.
- **Role mapping (a choice):** LW = Left Wing + Left Midfield, RW = Right Wing + Right Midfield; AM = Center / Left / Right Attacking Midfield; DM = the three Defensive Midfield slots; CM = the three Center Midfield slots; ST includes Secondary Striker.
- **Bars:** 30 = `MIN_COHORT_SIZE` (D11). Percentiles are local (per league, §12.1), so a role's own percentile cohort needs ≥ 30 in *every* league; pooled n is what a map (PCA) uses. 60 = the user's bar for a stable map.

## A combined "midfield" map: the inputs

- DM-CM rows: 294. AM-Winger rows with ≥ 0.7 of their minutes in central slots (DM + CM + AM): **26** of 277 (AM-labelled 10).
- Combined: 320 rows; per league La Liga 73, Ligue 1 82, Premier League 76, Serie A 89.
- DNA lists: 20 metrics shared; DM-CM only: final_third_entries_carry, np_xg, op_xg_assisted, team_pass_share; AM-Winger only: final_third_entries_pass, np_shots_on_target.
- The two groups' percentiles rank against different reference populations (each row against its own group in its league), so a DM-CM 90th percentile and an AM-Winger 90th percentile on the same metric are not the same value.

## Proposal

Proposal only, nothing implemented; a dated snapshot (2026-10-07) of the figures above. Its
quoted figures are checked on each run (PROPOSAL_FIGURES); a mismatch fails it.

- **No fine role gets its own percentile cohort.** Percentiles are local, so a cohort needs
  ≥ 30 in every league. Only CB meets it, and the CB role is 93% of the CB group already. LB
  (min 15), RB (16), DM (16, Premier League) and ST (28, Ligue 1) fail.
- **Own map (PCA of the unchanged group-percentile vectors, role rows only): none
  recommended now.** Pooled n ≥ 60 holds for CB 237, ST 140, DM 120, LB 92 and RB 83, but:
  - CB and ST are 93% and 89% of their groups, so their map is the group map;
  - LB vs RB is a mirror (side), not a style difference. A left-back-only map halves n and
    shows the same axes;
  - a DM-only map is the 6 side of the DM-CM map, where M4g1 found a continuum.
- **Colour / filter on the group map (recommended):**
  - FB-WB: LB / RB / LWB / RWB / mixed (LWB 5 and RWB 4 are too few to read alone);
  - DM-CM: DM / CM / mixed (CM 44);
  - AM-Winger: LW / RW / AM / mixed. AM is only 10 rows; central 10s are mostly mixed with a
    wing (AM/RW 23, AM/LW 19), and LW/RW is the largest combo (61);
  - CB and ST: colour the mixed rows only (CB/RB 12; RW/ST 9).
  - **Caveat:** LW / RW merge the wide forward slot (Left/Right Wing, 63–66% of minutes) with
    the wide midfield slot of a 4-4-2 (34–37%). Like 6 / 8, the slot records formation as well
    as role.
- **Combined "midfield" map: feasible by count, not by method, and of little gain.**
  - **Count:** 294 DM-CM + 26 central AM-Winger rows = 320 (73–89 per league).
  - **Method:** each group's percentiles rank against its own population, and the DNA lists
    share 20 metrics. A combined map would need the 320 rows re-ranked in one cohort, which
    is a cohort change (ADR), against "cohorts unchanged".
  - **Gain:** the added rows are 8% of the map.
  - **Recommendation:** not now. If wanted later, propose a "central midfield" cohort in its
    own ADR.
